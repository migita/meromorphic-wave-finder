Check a solution

Enter an equation and a candidate in closed form. The engine substitutes the candidate and reduces the result exactly. There are three possible answers: the residual is exactly zero, it is not zero, or the engine could not decide. The answer “exactly zero” rests on exact computation alone; a numerical residual, where one is shown, only illustrates the other two answers.

  • a double pole
  • the Weierstrass function
  • a front of the Kuramoto–Sivashinsky reduction

Functions: exp, tanh, tan, sqrt, …; Weierstrass functions wp(z, g2, g3) and wpp(z, g2, g3) (the derivative), with ℘′² = 4℘³ − g₂℘ − g₃. The imaginary unit is I.
